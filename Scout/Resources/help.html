✦ User Guide

Everything you need to
find any screenshot

Scout watches your folders and makes every screenshot instantly searchable — by the text inside it, by what you remember seeing, or both at once.

🚀

Getting started

1

Add a folder to watch

Click Choose Screenshots Folder… on the welcome screen, or go to Settings → Watched Folders → Add Folder. Scout will scan the folder immediately. macOS automatically saves screenshots to ~/Desktop — that's a good first choice.

2

Wait for indexing to finish

Scout reads the text in each image using OCR and builds a search index. A progress bar appears while this runs. For a few hundred screenshots it takes under a minute; larger libraries may take a few minutes. New screenshots are indexed automatically as they arrive.

3

Search anything

Type in the search bar — Scout finds screenshots containing that text or matching that meaning in real time. Press ↵ to open a screenshot full-size, or Space to Quick Look it without leaving the grid.

💡 You can add multiple folders — a work folder, a personal folder, a Downloads folder. Scout watches all of them at once.
🔍

Searching

🔤

Type anything you remember

Scout searches the text that was visible in the screenshot — error messages, code, a price, a username, a URL. Partial words work too.

✨

Describe what you saw

Switch to Meaning mode and describe the screenshot conceptually — "invoice from freelancer" even if the word "invoice" isn't in the image.

⚡

Results appear instantly

The grid updates as you type in Exact mode. Hit Enter or click Search to run a Meaning or Both search.

🔗

Find similar screenshots

Open a screenshot detail view and click Find Similar to surface other screenshots that are visually or topically related.

⚙️

Search modes

Aa Exact

Word for word

Finds screenshots containing the exact words you type. Uses full-text search with smart stemming — "running" matches "run".

Try searching
stripe payment failed
✦ Meaning

By meaning

Describes what you remember and finds conceptually related screenshots — even if none of those words appear in the image.

Try searching
chart showing user growth
⇄ Both

Both at once

Runs Exact and Meaning in parallel. Screenshots matching both float to the top. Casts the widest possible net.

Try searching
error loading module
ℹ️ Meaning mode requires the AI model to be bundled with Scout. If it's unavailable, Exact search is used as a fallback automatically.

Sidebar & filters

The left sidebar lets you browse your library without searching. Click any section to filter the grid instantly.

📷

All Screenshots

Every indexed screenshot across all watched folders, newest first.

📅

Date — Today / This Week / This Month / Custom Range

Filter by when the screenshot was taken. Custom Range lets you pick any start and end date.

📁

Watched Folders

Click a folder name to see only screenshots from that folder.

🏷️

Tags

Your manually added tags appear here. Click one to see all screenshots you've tagged with it.

🤖

Categories

Scout automatically classifies screenshots into categories like Code, Chat, Design, Meeting, Media, and Error. Click a category to browse it.

🗑️

Select & delete

1

Enter select mode

Long-press any screenshot card (hold ~0.45s) to enter select mode and select that card. Or click the Select button in the top-right of the grid.

2

Tap to add or remove

In select mode, tap any card to toggle its selection. A checkmark appears on selected cards. You can select as many as you like.

3

Delete selected items

Press ⌫ or click the Delete button that appears in the toolbar. Confirm the alert. Both the screenshot file on disk and Scout's index entry are removed.

⚠️ Deletion permanently removes the file from your disk — it does not go to the Trash. Use with care.
👁️

Quick Look preview

⎵

Press Space to preview

Press Space while hovering over a screenshot (or after navigating with arrow keys) to open it in Quick Look without leaving the grid.

↕

Navigate while previewing

Use ↑ ↓ arrow keys while Quick Look is open to step through your results one by one.

↵

Open full detail view

Press ↵ or click a card to open the full detail view with the large image, OCR text, tags, and a Find Similar button.

📄

OCR text in Finder too

The ScoutQL extension makes Quick Look in Finder show the extracted text alongside the image — works system-wide, no Scout window needed.

🏷️

Tags

Tags let you manually organise screenshots into named groups that persist across searches.

1

Open a screenshot

Click any screenshot card to open its detail view.

2

Add a tag

Type a tag name in the tag field and press ↵. Tags are lowercase and shared across all screenshots — use the same name on multiple screenshots to group them.

3

Browse by tag

Your tags appear in the sidebar under Tags. Click any tag to see all screenshots that carry it.

💡 Scout also auto-tags screenshots with categories like Code, Error, and Design — those appear under Categories in the sidebar, separate from your manual tags.
🧠

Smart clusters

After indexing, Scout groups your screenshots into topic clusters automatically — no tagging required.

⚡

Automatic grouping

Once indexing is done, Scout runs K-means clustering on the AI embeddings. Screenshots with similar content end up in the same cluster — a "Stripe receipts" cluster, a "Slack conversations" cluster, etc.

🔄

Refresh clusters

After adding many new screenshots, go to Settings → Re-cluster (or the cluster view header) to regroup with your updated library.

ℹ️ Clustering requires the Meaning AI model. If the model isn't bundled, the cluster browser is hidden.
⚡

Hotkey palette

⌥⇧⎵
Open Scout from anywhere
Press ⌥ Option + ⇧ Shift + Space from any app to instantly open the floating search palette.
🪟

Works from any app

The hotkey works globally — you don't need to switch to Scout first. Press it while in Xcode, a browser, or a document and the palette appears instantly.

🔑

Change the hotkey

Open Settings to reassign the hotkey to a combination that doesn't conflict with your other apps.

🔦

Spotlight integration

Every screenshot Scout indexes is also searchable from macOS Spotlight — no Scout window needed.

1

Press ⌘Space

Open Spotlight with the standard macOS shortcut.

2

Search for text in your screenshots

Type words you remember from inside a screenshot. Scout-indexed items appear in the Spotlight results list with the screenshot filename and the matched text.

3

Click the result

Clicking a Spotlight result opens Scout and highlights that screenshot directly. Scout handles the scout:// URL scheme to make this work.

Privacy

📦

Everything is local

OCR, semantic search, and AI classification all run on your Mac. Your screenshots never leave your device.

🌐

No network requests

Scout makes zero network calls during normal operation. It doesn't send telemetry, usage data, or crash reports anywhere.

💾

Your data location

Everything Scout stores lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Scout/ — delete that folder to wipe all Scout data.

🗑️

Uninstalling

Menu bar icon → Uninstall Scout…, or About → Uninstall…. Type REMOVE to confirm. You can optionally delete all data at the same time.
